Delivery & Returns Policy

Simple and clear rules about how your cards are delivered, how to find lost cards, and how to get a replacement if a card has an issue.

Instant 100% Digital Delivery | No Waiting for Post Mail | 100% Safe Stock

1. 100% Instant Digital Delivery

All products sold on CheckerPay Ghana are electronic examination vouchers (Serial Numbers and confidential PINs). We do not ship physical scratch cards via courier or postal mail.

1. Instant On-Screen Display

The second your Mobile Money or card payment is approved, your Serial Number and PIN show up on your screen with a 1-click copy button and a link to the official exam portal.

2. Instant SMS Text Message

We also send a text message with your Order Reference, Serial Number, and PIN straight to the phone number you entered at checkout.

3. Delivery Speed

You will see your card in under 5 seconds after payment. On busy exam release days with lots of telecom traffic, it can take up to 30 to 60 seconds.

2. Self-Service Voucher Recovery (Lost or Closed Tabs)

If your phone battery died, your internet went off, or you closed your tab before writing down your PIN, you can find your card anytime without waiting:

How to Find Your Lost Card:
  1. Visit our recovery page at checkerpay.com.gh/lookup.
  2. Type the Mobile Money phone number used during purchase or your Order Reference (like ORD-2026...).
  3. Your order details and cards will show up on screen immediately.

3. Returns & Refund Restrictions for Digital Goods

Under Section 38 of the Ghana Electronic Transactions Act, 2008 (Act 772), cancellation rights do not apply to digital items once they are sent to the customer with their consent.

Because a voucher PIN is a private code that cannot be taken back once shown, completed voucher purchases are final and non-refundable, unless the card has a proven defect as explained below.

4. 100% Replacement Guarantee for Defective Vouchers

We guarantee that every voucher sold is fresh and working. In the rare case that a voucher fails on an official examination portal:

What Counts as a Defective Card:

  • The official portal shows: "Card Already Used" or "Invalid Serial/PIN" on your very first try.
  • The PIN or Serial number format does not match official rules.

How to Get a Replacement:

  1. Take a clear, full screenshot of the official portal showing the error message and the codes you typed.
  2. Send your Order Reference, Serial Number, and the screenshot to our support team on WhatsApp or email.
  3. We check the logs against portal timestamps. Once confirmed as a faulty card, a fresh replacement voucher is issued immediately for free.
Note: Vouchers that were already checked 3 times by the student or locked to another student's index number by mistake do not qualify for replacement.

5. Duplicate Telecom Debits & Failed Orders

If a network delay causes your Mobile Money account to be debited twice for a single order:

  • Our computer automatically finds duplicate payments for the same order reference.
  • If money was taken from you without a voucher being given, our system will either deliver your voucher right away or trigger a telecom refund.
  • Refunds are paid straight back to your Mobile Money account within 24 to 48 hours.
