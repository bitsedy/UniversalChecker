Delivery & Returns Policy

Clear guidelines on instant digital delivery, voucher recovery, and replacement guarantees.

1. 100% Instant Digital Delivery

All examination vouchers are electronic codes delivered immediately on screen and via SMS.

1. On-Screen Display

Your Serial and PIN appear immediately once payment is approved.

2. SMS Text Message

Order reference and voucher details are sent directly to your phone.

3. Delivery Speed

Delivered in under 5 seconds with zero postal waiting time.

2. Self-Service Voucher Recovery

Easily retrieve your purchased cards if you closed your browser tab.

  1. Visit our recovery tool at checkerpay.com.gh/lookup.
  2. Enter your Mobile Money phone number or Order Reference.
  3. View and copy your vouchers instantly on screen.

3. Digital Goods Returns Policy

Under Section 38 of Ghana's Electronic Transactions Act (Act 772), digital codes cannot be returned once revealed. Completed voucher purchases are final and non-refundable unless the card has a proven defect.

4. 100% Replacement Guarantee for Defective Vouchers

We guarantee that every voucher sold is authentic, unused, and working.

Eligible Defective Conditions:

  • Portal returns "Card Already Used" or "Invalid PIN" on your first attempt.
  • PIN or Serial format fails official exam portal validation rules.
How to Request Replacement: Send your Order Reference and a screenshot of the portal message to support. Confirmed defective cards are replaced immediately for free.

5. Duplicate Telecom Debits

Our system automatically reconciles duplicate debits for the same order reference. Confirmed over-deductions are refunded to your mobile money wallet within 24 to 48 hours.
