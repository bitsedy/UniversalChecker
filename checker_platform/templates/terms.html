2. Nature of Digital Vouchers & Instant Fulfillment

CheckerPay Ghana is an authorized digital reseller of electronic scratch card vouchers (including WAEC WASSCE, BECE, CSSPS School Placement, and CTVET Examination vouchers).

  • Immediate Delivery: All vouchers are digital codes. The moment your Mobile Money or card payment is approved, your Serial Number and confidential PIN are immediately displayed on your screen and dispatched via SMS.
  • One-Time Allocation: Each voucher code is unique and reserved only for your order using atomic safe locking. Once shown on your screen or sent to your phone, your order is complete.
  • Single-Candidate Binding: As required by WAEC and CSSPS portal rules, the first time you check results on an official portal, that voucher binds permanently to the student's candidate index number. 4. Refund & Replacement Policy

Under Section 38 of the Electronic Transactions Act (Act 772), digital codes that have already been shown or opened cannot be returned.

Because an examination voucher PIN is a confidential code that cannot be taken back once shown, purchased vouchers are strictly non-refundable once revealed, except if the card has a proven technical defect (such as showing "Already Used" before your first try).
