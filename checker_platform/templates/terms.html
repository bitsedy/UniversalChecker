2. Nature of Digital Vouchers & Instant Fulfillment

CheckerPay Ghana operates as an authorized secondary digital reseller of electronic scratch card vouchers (including WAEC WASSCE, BECE, CSSPS School Placement, and CTVET Examination vouchers).

  • Immediate Delivery: Electronic vouchers are intangible digital assets. Upon successful Mobile Money or payment authorization, the Serial Number and confidential PIN are immediately displayed on your screen and dispatched via SMS.
  • One-Time Allocation: Each voucher code is cryptographically unique and reserved exclusively for your order using atomic inventory locking. Once revealed or sent to your designated phone number, the transaction is deemed fully executed and delivered.

CheckerPay has no authority or technological capability to unbind or transfer a card to another candidate once submitted.

4. Refund & Replacement Policy

Under Section 38 of the Electronic Transactions Act (Act 772), the standard right of return does not apply to digitally supplied goods that have been accessed or unsealed by the consumer.

Consequently, purchased vouchers are strictly non-refundable once revealed, except in the specific instance of a verified technical defect (e.g., a card showing "Already Used" prior to the customer's initial lookup). Detailed procedures for defect remediation are outlined in our Delivery & Returns Policy.
