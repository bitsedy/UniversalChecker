Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)

Privacy Policy

CheckerPay Ghana is committed to safeguarding the personal and educational data privacy of all Ghanaian students, parents, and platform users.

Effective Date: September 2026 | Certified Privacy & Zero-Persistence Architecture

1. Legislative Framework & Data Controller Identity

This Privacy Policy explains how CheckerPay Ghana ("Data Controller") collects, processes, and safeguards personal data in strict compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana.

As a digital reseller and educational advisory portal, we adhere to the eight core principles of data protection set out under Section 17 of Act 843: accountability, lawfulness, purpose specification, information quality, security safeguards, data subject participation, and openness.

2. What Information We Collect

A. Transactional Information (For Order Fulfillment)

When you purchase a voucher, we collect your Mobile Money phone number, network operator (MTN, Telecel, AT), customer name (if provided), and IP address. This information is required to process payment with your telecom provider, issue SMS delivery, and enable self-service retrieval at /lookup.

B. Educational Advisor Inputs (Zero-Persistence Processing)

When you use the Placement & Pathway Advisor, you supply subject grades (e.g. Core Mathematics, English, Integrated Science, and electives). Under Section 20 of Act 843, this processing requires your explicit affirmative consent.

C. Customer Feedback

When you submit a review or feedback form, we store your rating, category, message, and submission timestamp to monitor and enhance service quality.

3. Zero-Persistence Guarantee for Academic Records

Strictly Ephemeral In-Memory Calculation

CheckerPay does NOT store student transcripts, index numbers, candidate photographs, or individual subject grades on persistent storage or databases. Once the algorithm finishes computing your Best-6 aggregate, institutional chances, and verdict, the data is instantly purged from volatile server memory (RAM).

4. Lawful Basis for Processing (Section 20 of Act 843)

We process your data strictly under the following lawful conditions:

  • Contractual Necessity: Delivering purchased voucher PINs and sending SMS fulfillment messages.
  • Explicit Consent: Explicit user checkbox opt-in prior to processing grades in the Pathway Advisor.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Maintaining audit trails and financial transaction ledgers as required by Ghanaian financial transaction laws.

5. Security Safeguards & Anti-Scraping Protection

In compliance with Section 28 of Act 843, we implement state-of-the-art security measures to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of personal data:

  • Credential Masking: When searching by phone number on our voucher recovery tool, voucher PINs are automatically masked (e.g. ••••••••1234) to protect credentials from shoulder-surfing and unauthorized snooping.
  • Transport Security: All data is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Cryptographic Audit Ledger: Administrative actions are immutably logged into a SHA-256 block-hash ledger to guarantee accountability.

6. Data Subject Rights Under Ghana Act 843

Under Sections 24, 28, and 35 of the Data Protection Act, you possess the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether we hold transaction records associated with your phone number.
  • Right to Correction: You may request correction of erroneous customer information.
  • Right to Cease Processing: You may object to the processing of personal data for direct marketing purposes (we do not sell or trade phone numbers to third parties).
