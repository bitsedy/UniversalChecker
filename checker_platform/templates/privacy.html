Privacy Policy

CheckerPay Ghana promises to keep all personal details and student information safe and private.

Updated: September 2026 | 100% Private | Zero-Persistence Guarantee for Academic Records

1. Ghana Privacy Laws & Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how CheckerPay Ghana collects, uses, and protects your information under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of Ghana.

As an official card reseller and school guide, we follow all rules in Section 17 of Act 843: being honest, following the law, keeping your information safe, and respecting your privacy.

2. What Information We Collect

A. Order & Payment Details

When you buy a voucher card, we ask for your Mobile Money phone number, your network (MTN, Telecel, or AT), your name (if you want to share it), and your internet IP address. We need this so your network can process payment, so we can send your SMS text, and so you can find your card at /lookup.

B. School Guidance Inputs

When you use the Placement & Pathway Advisor, you type in your exam grades (like Core Math, English, Science, and other subjects). Under Section 20 of Act 843, we only check your grades if you check the agreement box first.

C. Customer Feedback

When you send a message or give us a star rating, we save your message and rating to make our service better.

3. Zero-Persistence Guarantee for Academic Records

We Never Save Your School Grades (Wiped Right Away)

CheckerPay does NOT save student report cards, index numbers, student photos, or subject grades to any hard drive or database. As soon as the computer finishes counting your best 6 subjects and showing your matches, your grades are completely wiped clean from server memory.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing (Section 20 of Act 843)

We only look at your data for these clear reasons:

  • Sending Your Voucher: Showing your voucher PIN on screen and sending it by SMS text message.
  • Your Clear Permission: You check the box before the Pathway Advisor checks your grades.
  • Ghana Law Rules: Keeping basic payment records as required by Ghana financial transaction laws.

5. How We Keep Your Information Safe

Following Section 28 of Act 843, we use strong safety locks to protect your details:

  • Hidden Secret Numbers: When you look up a lost voucher using your phone number, the secret PIN numbers are hidden (like ••••••••1234) so strangers looking over your shoulder cannot steal them.
  • Safe Web Lock: All information is scrambled and locked safely as it travels over the web (using TLS 1.3 encryption).
  • Tamper-Proof Records: System changes are saved into a permanent security ledger so nobody can alter records secretly.

6. Your Rights Under Ghana Act 843

Under Sections 24, 28, and 35 of the Data Protection Act, you have these rights:

  • Right to Ask: You can ask what payment records we have for your phone number.
  • Right to Fix: You can ask us to fix any wrong customer information.
  • No Spam: We never sell, rent, or give your phone number to strangers for ads.
