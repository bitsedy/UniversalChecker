Privacy Policy

Compliant with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) to safeguard your personal details.

1. Data Protection Compliance

CheckerPay Ghana protects your personal information following the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). We process data transparently and lawfully to deliver your examination vouchers securely.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only minimal information necessary to fulfill your orders.

A. Order & Payment Details

Phone number, mobile network, and transaction reference needed for payment and SMS delivery.

B. School Guidance Inputs

Candidate grades are evaluated only with your consent under Section 20 of Act 843.

C. Customer Feedback

Ratings and comments shared voluntarily to improve platform reliability and service.

3. Zero-Persistence Guarantee for Academic Records

CheckerPay never stores candidate grades, index numbers, or academic transcripts to any database or disk. All grade calculations occur strictly in ephemeral memory and are wiped immediately upon completion.

4. Lawful Processing Grounds

Data is processed strictly under authorized legal grounds:

  • Voucher fulfillment and instant SMS text dispatch.
  • Explicit candidate consent prior to running the Pathway Advisor.
  • Statutory compliance with Bank of Ghana transaction record rules.

5. Security Safeguards

We deploy enterprise encryption and audit logging to prevent unauthorized access:

  • Masked voucher PINs on public telephone lookup queries.
  • End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption across all network communication.
  • Cryptographic ledger ensuring tamper-evident transaction logs.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Under Act 843, you have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of personal data. For privacy inquiries, please contact our Data Protection & Compliance Office at privacy@checkerpay.com.gh.
