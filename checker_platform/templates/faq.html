Vouchers, Authenticity & Usage

Are CheckerPay vouchers authentic and accepted on official portals? +

Yes, absolutely 100%. All vouchers sold on CheckerPay are officially issued batches sourced directly through accredited educational distribution agreements. They are fully recognized and validated on ghana.waecdirect.org, cssps.gov.gh, and ctvet.gov.gh.

How many times can I use a WAEC Result Checker card? +

A WAEC voucher allows a strict maximum of 3 successful lookups for one specific candidate. The moment you enter your Index Number and check your result, the card is permanently locked to your index number. It cannot be used for any other student.

What is the difference between WASSCE School and WASSCE Private? +

WASSCE (School) is for candidates who wrote their examination through an accredited senior high school (regular May/June session).
WASSCE (Private) is for candidates who registered independently as private candidates (often called NOV/DEC). On the WAEC portal, make sure you select the matching exam type dropdown.

Mobile Money & Payments

Which payment methods are supported? +

We accept all Ghanaian Mobile Money wallets: MTN Mobile Money (*170#), Telecel Cash (*110#), and AT Money (*110#). We also support debit/credit cards (Visa and Mastercard) through our secure Paystack checkout.

My MoMo was debited, but I closed the tab before copying my PIN. What do I do? +

Don't panic! Your voucher was automatically saved. Simply go to our Voucher Recovery Page, type in your Mobile Money phone number or Order Reference, and your complete Serial Number and PIN will be displayed immediately.

How long does the payment approval prompt take to appear on my phone? +

Usually within 5 to 15 seconds. If you are using Telecel Cash or MTN MoMo and the prompt does not pop up automatically, dial *170# → Option 6 (My Wallet) → Option 3 (Approvals) to approve the pending transaction manually.

Digital Delivery & Voucher Recovery

How quickly will I receive my Serial Number and PIN? +

Instantaneous (under 3 seconds). As soon as your Mobile Money authorization is confirmed, the Serial Number and PIN appear right on your device screen with a 1-click copy button. An SMS receipt containing the details is also dispatched to the phone number you provided.

What should I do if a PIN shows "Card Already Used" on the WAEC portal? +

Because our inventory is concurrency-locked, pre-used cards are virtually unheard of. However, if this rare circumstance occurs, contact our support immediately with your Order Reference and a screenshot of the error. Once verified against our telemetry logs, an instant replacement voucher will be issued immediately under our Delivery & Returns SLA.

Placement & Pathway Advisor

How does the Pathway Advisor compute school and university chances? +

The Pathway Advisor calculates your Best-6 aggregate according to Ghanaian standards (Core Math, English, Science/Social Studies + 3 best electives for WASSCE; 4 cores + 2 best electives for BECE). It then matches your profile against real historical cut-off aggregates across UG Legon, KNUST, UCC, UPSA, and Category A/B/C SHS institutions, providing realistic acceptance probabilities (High, Moderate, Reach).

Is my student examination data recorded or stored? +

No. In strict compliance with Section 20 of the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), all grades and subject entries are processed exclusively in volatile computer memory (RAM) and vaporized immediately once your advice report is returned. We do not store your grades.
