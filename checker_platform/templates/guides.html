How to Check Official Results Without Burning Your Voucher

Ghana exam websites have strict rules. Read this easy guide so you do not waste your checks or get an error.

1. WAEC Portal (WASSCE & BECE)

Open ghana.waecdirect.org ↗

The official WAEC Ghana website checks BECE and WASSCE results for both school and private candidates.

The 3-Check Limit & Index Binding

Each WAEC card lets you check results 3 times only. On the 4th try, the card stops working. Also, the card locks to the first index number you type. You cannot give it to someone else.

The "Voucher Burn" Risk

WAEC counts 1 check the moment you click Submit. If your internet is slow while waiting, DO NOT refresh or reload the page. Refreshing burns another check instantly. Wait patiently or take a screenshot as soon as your results show.

Step-by-Step WAEC Instructions:

  1. Go to ghana.waecdirect.org.
  2. Type your 10-digit Index Number (for example: 0010101010).
  3. Pick your Exam Type: WASSCE (School), WASSCE (Private), BECE (School), or BECE (Private).
  4. Pick your Exam Year (for example: 2026).
  5. Type the Card Serial Number and 12-digit PIN you bought on CheckerPay.
  6. Click Submit and wait. When it opens, click Print or save it as a PDF right away.

2. CSSPS Senior High School Placement

Open cssps.gov.gh ↗

The CSSPS website checks Senior High School (SHS) and Technical / Vocational school placement for BECE students.

The 10 vs 12-Digit Index Rule (Most Common Failure)

WAEC uses 10 numbers. But the CSSPS placement website strictly requires you to add your 2-digit exam year to the end of your 10-digit index number (12 numbers in total).

Invalid: 1010101010 (Only 10 digits) → Fails validation
Valid: 101010101026 (12 digits for 2026 completion)

Unlimited Placement Checks

Unlike WAEC cards, one CSSPS card lets you check your placement as many times as you like for that student. You can check again anytime.

Self-Placement Choice Lock

If the computer does not place you in a school and you choose one yourself (Self-Placement), your choice is saved forever once you submit. Choose your school and boarder/day status very carefully!

3. CTVET / NABPTEX / TEU Portal

Open ctvet.gov.gh ↗

CTVET checks technical, vocational, and artisan exam certificates and results.

Regional & Center Code Routing

Make sure you know your Exam Series (May/June or Nov/Dec), your Region, and your school's Center Code before checking. If you enter the wrong center code, the page will be blank.
