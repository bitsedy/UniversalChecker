1. WAEC Direct Portal (WASSCE & BECE)

Open ghana.waecdirect.org ↗

The official WAEC Ghana Direct website (ghana.waecdirect.org) authenticates both school and private candidate results for the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE).

Official Rule Specification • WAEC Ghana

The 3-Check Limit & Index Binding: What is the WAEC Rule?

In Ghana, every valid WAEC result checker voucher allows a strict maximum of 3 checks. On the very first check, WAEC's authentication database permanently binds the voucher's serial and PIN to that candidate's 10-digit Index Number and Exam Year. The card cannot be transferred, shared, or used to view results for any other candidate. On the fourth verification attempt, the voucher is rendered permanently invalid.

Anti-Burn Protocol • Critical Advisory

Why does "voucher burn" happen and how can candidates avoid it?

WAEC registers a consumed check the exact millisecond you click Submit. If your local internet lags or the portal server hesitates, DO NOT refresh, reload, or click back in your browser. Doing so sends a duplicate request that burns another check instantly. When the result statement renders on your screen, click Print or save as PDF immediately to ensure you never need to burn subsequent checks for official submissions.

Step-by-Step WAEC Portal Verification:

  1. Open your browser and navigate directly to ghana.waecdirect.org.
  2. Enter your exact 10-digit Index Number (e.g., 0010101010). Do not add any spaces, letters, or exam year numbers.
  3. Select your Type of Examination: WASSCE (School), WASSCE (Private), BECE (School), or BECE (Private).
  4. Select your Exam Year (e.g., 2026).
  5. Carefully input the Serial Number and 12-digit PIN received on your CheckerPay voucher slip.
  6. Click Submit once. Wait patiently for the portal to generate your official statement of result, then save or print as PDF right away.

2. CSSPS Senior High School Placement Portal

Open cssps.gov.gh ↗

The Computerized School Selection and Placement System (cssps.gov.gh) manages placement into Senior High Schools (SHS), Senior High Technical Schools (SHTS), and Technical & Vocational Education and Training (TVET) institutions across Ghana.

Mandatory Index Structure • CSSPS Placement

The 10 vs 12-Digit Index Rule: Why does the CSSPS Placement portal require 12 digits?

While WAEC uses a 10-digit index, the CSSPS portal strictly mandates a 12-digit format by requiring candidates to append their 2-digit BECE completion year to the end of their 10-digit index number. Entering only 10 digits triggers an immediate validation failure.

✕ Invalid: 1010101010 (10 digits — rejects with "Invalid Index Number")
✓ Valid: 101010101026 (12 digits for candidate completing BECE in 2026)
Voucher Lifecycle • Placement Guarantees

Can a CSSPS placement checker voucher be used more than once?

Yes. Unlike WAEC result cards which expire after 3 checks, a single CSSPS placement voucher allows unlimited checks for that specific candidate throughout the active placement admissions cycle. Parents and candidates may log in repeatedly to check status updates, print enrollment forms, or verify boarding status.

Important Warning on Self-Placement: If the automated system does not place a student automatically and you use the Self-Placement module to select a school, your chosen school and residential status (Day vs Boarding) become permanently locked upon confirmation. Make sure of your selection before submitting.

Step-by-Step CSSPS Placement Access:

  1. Visit the official placement portal at cssps.gov.gh.
  2. Enter your 12-digit Index Number by appending the 2-digit completion year (e.g. 001010101026 for 2026).
  3. Enter the Voucher PIN and Serial Number obtained from CheckerPay.
  4. Click Submit to retrieve your official placement form. Download and print the placement notification for school registration.

Authoritative Notice on CTVET / NABPTEX / TEU Results

Institutional Policy • Anti-Scam Guidance

Does Commission for TVET (CTVET) sell online result checker vouchers?

No. The Commission for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (CTVET), incorporating the former NABPTEX and Technical Examinations Unit (TEU), does not issue or sell scratch cards, serial PINs, or online vouchers for public result verification. Candidates who sat for National Certificate, Intermediate, Advanced, Diploma, or Technician examinations must collect their official statement of results and certificates directly in person from their respective technical institutes or approved examination centers. Never purchase third-party scratch cards claiming to check CTVET results online.
