1. WAEC Direct Portal Guide

Open ghana.waecdirect.org ↗

The official WAEC Ghana Direct website authenticates both school and private candidate results for BECE and WASSCE.

Official Rule Specification • WAEC Ghana

The 3-Check Limit & Index Binding: What is the WAEC Rule?

In Ghana, every valid WAEC voucher allows a strict maximum of 3 checks. On the first check, WAEC binds the voucher permanently to that candidate's 10-digit Index Number and exam year.

Anti-Burn Protocol • Critical Advisory

How to Avoid Burning Your Voucher

Never refresh or click back while the portal is loading, as every click consumes a check. When results display, print or save as PDF immediately to conserve your checks.

Step-by-Step WAEC Verification:

  1. Open your browser and navigate directly to ghana.waecdirect.org.
  2. Enter your exact 10-digit Index Number without spaces or exam year numbers.
  3. Select your Type of Examination and your Exam Year.
  4. Input the Serial Number and 12-digit PIN from your voucher.
  5. Click Submit once and save your result slip as PDF right away.

2. CSSPS Senior High School Placement

Open cssps.gov.gh ↗

The CSSPS portal (cssps.gov.gh) manages placement into Senior High Schools and TVET institutions across Ghana.

Mandatory Index Structure • CSSPS Placement

The 10 vs 12-Digit Index Rule: Why does the CSSPS Placement portal require 12 digits?

CSSPS mandates a 12-digit format by appending your 2-digit completion year to your 10-digit index number. Entering only 10 digits triggers an immediate validation failure.

✕ Invalid: 1010101010 (10 digits — rejects with error)
✓ Valid: 101010101026 (12 digits for BECE 2026)
Voucher Lifecycle • Placement Guarantees

Can a CSSPS placement checker voucher be used more than once?

Yes, a single CSSPS voucher allows unlimited checks for that candidate throughout the active admissions cycle. If using self-placement, choose carefully because selected schools lock permanently upon confirmation.

Step-by-Step CSSPS Placement Access:

  1. Visit the official placement portal at cssps.gov.gh.
  2. Enter your 12-digit Index Number by appending the 2-digit completion year (e.g. 001010101026).
  3. Enter the Voucher PIN and Serial Number obtained from CheckerPay.
  4. Click Submit to retrieve and print your official placement form.

Notice on CTVET Examination Results

Institutional Policy • Anti-Scam Guidance

Does Commission for TVET (CTVET) sell online result checker vouchers?

No. The Commission for TVET (CTVET) does not issue or sell online scratch cards or PINs. Candidates must collect their official statement of results directly in person from their technical institute.
