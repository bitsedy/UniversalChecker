How to Check Official Results Without Burning Your Voucher

Ghanaian examination portals have strict validation and usage mechanics. Read this guide to ensure you do not exhaust attempts or fail validation.

🎓

1. WAEC Portal (WASSCE & BECE)

Open ghana.waecdirect.org ↗

The official WAEC Ghana portal checks both Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) and West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) for School and Private cohorts.

⚠️ The 3-Check Limit & Index Binding

Each WAEC voucher permits a strict maximum of 3 successful lookups. On the 4th attempt, the card is invalidated. Furthermore, the voucher permanently binds to the first candidate index number entered; it cannot be transferred to another student.

⚠️ The "Voucher Burn" Risk

WAEC decrements an attempt the moment the form is submitted. If your network lags while the result page loads, DO NOT refresh the browser. Refreshing burns another check instantly. Wait patiently or take a screenshot immediately once displayed.

Step-by-Step WAEC Instructions:

  1. Navigate to ghana.waecdirect.org.
  2. Enter your 10-digit Candidate Index Number (e.g. 0010101010).
  3. Select Examination Type: WASSCE (School), WASSCE (Private), BECE (School), or BECE (Private).
  4. Select your Examination Year (e.g., 2026).
  5. Enter the Card Serial Number and 12-digit PIN purchased from CheckerPay.
  6. Click Submit and wait. Once loaded, immediately click Print or save as PDF.
🏫

2. CSSPS Senior High School Placement

Open cssps.gov.gh ↗

The Computerized School Selection and Placement System (CSSPS) checks Senior High School (SHS) and Technical/Vocational placements for BECE candidates.

🚨 The 10 vs 12-Digit Index Rule (Most Common Failure)

Unlike WAEC which accepts a pure 10-digit index, CSSPS strictly requires candidates to append their 2-digit completion year to their index number.

❌ Wrong: 1010101010 (10 digits) → Fails validation
✓ Correct: 101010101026 (12 digits for 2026 completion)

Unlimited Placement Checks

Unlike WAEC cards, a CSSPS eVoucher allows unlimited status lookups for that single candidate, so parents and students can re-check placement forms as needed.

Self-Placement Choice Lock

If you are not automatically placed and enter the Self-Placement module, chosen schools lock permanently upon submission. Choose your programme and residential status carefully!

🔧

3. CTVET / NABPTEX / TEU Portal

Open ctvet.gov.gh ↗

The Commission for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (CTVET) handles technical, vocational, and artisan diploma and certificate results.

Regional & Center Code Routing

Ensure you know your Examination Series (May/June Certificate or Nov/Dec Technical), Region, and Center Code before attempting to check results. Submitting with an incorrect center code will result in an empty record.
