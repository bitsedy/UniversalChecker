For WASSCE and BECE: Enter your 10-digit Index Number (like 0010123456) and pick your exam year from the box.

For CSSPS High School Placement: You must use 12 numbers. Take your 10-digit index number and add your 2-digit exam year to the end (for example, index 0010123456 in year 2026 becomes 001012345626).

Simple Rule: Use 10 digits for WAEC. Use 12 digits for CSSPS School Placement.
