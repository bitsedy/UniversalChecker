Under official WAEC regulations, each result checker voucher allows a maximum of 3 checks for one specific candidate index number. The first time you check your results, WAEC's portal permanently binds that voucher's serial number to your candidate index number.

Avoid Burning Checks: Always take a screenshot, save a PDF, or print your statement of results during your very first check so you don't exhaust remaining attempts on simple re-views.

For WASSCE and BECE, enter your standard 10-digit Index Number (e.g. 0010123456) and select your examination year from the portal's dropdown menu.

For CSSPS SHS Placement, the Ministry of Education requires a 12-digit index by appending your 2-digit completion year directly to your index (e.g. Index 0010123456 completed in 2026 becomes 001012345626).

Rule of Thumb: WAEC portals require 10 digits; CSSPS placement portal requires 12 digits.
